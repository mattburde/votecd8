About this map

All three county boundaries are each county's own official GIS data.

Data date: Sept 6, 2026. CD-8 boundary: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line, 119th Congress (2025 vintage), Colorado. Adams County boundary: Adams County Colorado Government official GIS layer. Larimer County boundary: dissolved from Larimer County's own Board of County Commissioners (BOCC) district layer. Weld County boundary: Weld County official GIS open-data boundary layer. Basemap: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Drop-box counts sourced from each county clerk's official elections page; Adams/Larimer point coordinates from each county's own Google My Maps export; Weld point coordinates supplied by user, not independently verified. Address search: Nominatim (geocoding) + OSRM (driving directions), both free public OSM-based services.
